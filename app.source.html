Blast Radius · Entra app permission analyzer

Why this exists. In October 2026 a stolen vendor credential let a Microsoft 365 backup app's identity be used to sign in to more than 160 tenants, and in at least one a related management app made itself Global Administrator and planted secrets on other apps. Most teams had no idea an app could do that. This tool turns a permission list into a plain answer: if this app gets popped, what can the attacker reach, and can they take over the tenant? Map a partner's cross-tenant reach (GDAP)

      
Under GDAP, a partner-tenant security group is mapped to Entra roles scoped into each customer tenant, so whoever sits in that group, or steals its credential, can act in every customer it reaches. Paste a read-only export of the partner's GDAP relationships to see which customers a single compromise unlocks, and which groups are the master keys. Browser-only.
