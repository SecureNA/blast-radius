Blast Radius · Entra app permission analyzer

Why this exists. In October 2026 a stolen vendor credential let a Microsoft 365 backup app's identity be used across hundreds of tenants, and in at least one a related management app made itself Global Administrator and planted secrets on other apps. Most teams had no idea an app could do that. This tool turns a permission list into a plain answer: if this app gets popped, what can the attacker reach, and can they take over the tenant?
